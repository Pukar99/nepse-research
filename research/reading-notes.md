# Reading notes

Read every paper in three passes (S. Keshav, *How to Read a Paper*):

1. **Pass 1 (10 min):** title, abstract, headings, figures, conclusion. What kind of paper is it, and is it worth pass 2?
2. **Pass 2 (1 hour):** read it properly, skip the proofs. Understand every figure. Mark what you don't understand.
3. **Pass 3 (only for the papers your work builds on):** rebuild it in your head, or in code.
